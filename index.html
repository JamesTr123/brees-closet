A little help getting around

Common Questions

How do I log what I wore?

Tap Log an Outfit on Home or in My Outfits. Select a saved outfit or tap Put Together an Outfit, choose the date, and log it. A new combination is also saved to My Outfits.

What does the Match meter mean?

It shows recommendation strength from 0 to 100: blue is lower and red is higher. Weather fit, favorites, similarity, colors, and wear history affect the score. It measures the recommendation, not warmth.

Why is Home's score different from my filtered results?

Home checks any style and avoids outfits worn in the last seven days. It uses today's loaded weather, or any warmth until weather is loaded. Your Outfit Decider filters may narrow that list.

Why am I not getting suggestions?

Check that you have available tops and bottoms with compatible styles. Items in laundry, dismissed combinations, warmth choices, and recent-wear filters can reduce the results. Try a broader warmth range or change the repeat filter.

How do I see only new or saved outfits?

Tap Suggest Outfits, then use ALL, My Saved Outfits, or New Combinations at the top. They show up to eight, five, or three outfits respectively, strongest first.

Why is Laundry Swipe Mode empty?

Swipe Mode checks clothing logged in the last four calendar days. Log an outfit first, or use Update Laundry in Grid Mode to check any clothing. Swipe left for laundry and right for clean.

Can I use my closet on another device?

Sign in with the same email and password. Your cloud closet belongs to that account. Return to the app or refresh to load changes made on another device.

Does using a photo in several outfits use extra storage?

Outfits reference their clothing items, so showing the same clothing photo in several outfits doesn't create extra photo files. New outfit selfies do use additional space.

What does Free Up Photo Space remove?

It removes older cloud photos that no clothing, outfit, or history record uses. Photos uploaded within the last 24 hours are kept. Run it while the same account isn't being edited on another device.

What do backup and restore do?

Backup downloads clothing, outfits, wear history, dismissed suggestions, and their photos into a JSON file. Restore adds that data to the signed-in closet; matching record IDs use the backup details, and other records stay. It doesn't restore an account password or change your name.

Keep your closet safe

Photo Cleanup

Remove photos that your closet and wear history no longer use.

Run while this account isn't being edited on another device. Photos uploaded within the last 24 hours are kept.

A Copy for Safekeeping

Your cloud saves work across devices. A downloaded backup gives you a separate copy to keep.

Delete Your Account?

This permanently removes your account, cloud photos, clothes, outfits, wear history, and dismissed suggestions.

Download a backup first if you want to keep a copy. Backup files already downloaded are not deleted.
